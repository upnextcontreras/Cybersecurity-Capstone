## Project Phases

### Phase 2: Cybersecurity Policy Redefinition

**Objective:** Analyze, redefine, and modernize ACME's cybersecurity policies to align with current industry standards (GDPR, CCPA, NIST frameworks).

#### Key Activities:
* Review existing cybersecurity, password, and privacy policies.
* Identify whether policies should be updated, removed, or kept.
* Draft new versions of each critical policy.
* Include justifications, best practices, and scholarly citations.
* Develop a memo to management justifying policy changes.
* Craft an implementation plan addressing:
  * Policy enforcement methods
  * Strategies to ensure organization-wide awareness

#### Deliverables:
* Memo Document to John Kasket (2–3 pages, APA style)
* Updated Cybersecurity Policy
* Updated Password Policy
* Updated Privacy Policy
* APA-formatted references
